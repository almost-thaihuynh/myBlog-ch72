Throttle & Whiskers, Life beyond the Road.

Ride. Relax. Repeat.

Welcome to Two Wheels & Four Paws, a small look into two things that bring a lot of enjoyment to my life: my motorcycle and my cat. They may seem like two completely different interests, but each represents a different side of how I like to spend my time.

Riding my motorcycle gives me a sense of freedom and excitement that is hard to find anywhere else. Whether it is going for a quick ride, taking the long way home, or simply enjoying the sound and feel of the bike, riding gives me an opportunity to clear my head and enjoy the moment. Motorcycles have been an interest of mine for years, and my Ducati Panigale V2 has become one of my favorite ways to enjoy that passion.

When I’m not out riding, life slows down quite a bit. That’s where my cat comes in. Like most cats, there is plenty of personality, curiosity, and the occasional bit of chaos involved. Having a cat around provides a completely different kind of enjoyment from riding a motorcycle, but that’s part of what makes the combination interesting.

This website is dedicated to those two sides of my life. Throughout the page, I’ll share a little more about my motorcycle, why I enjoy riding, and the four-legged companion who makes life at home a little more entertaining.
